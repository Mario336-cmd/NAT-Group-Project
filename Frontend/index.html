How the application works

TrueCheck helps you assess factual claims using Wikipedia evidence, a BERT prediction and an explanation with sources.

Type a claim, upload an image or PDF, or use voice input. Review your results, listen to explanations and download PDF or Word reports.

TrueCheck searches a saved Wikipedia collection. Review the evidence carefully, as model predictions can be wrong.
